Aap ke naam, dil se

Mere pyare bhai,

Aaj aap ka birthday hai, aur mera dil chahta tha ke aap ko ek aisa tohfa doon jo aap hamesha yaad rakhein. Isi liye ye chhoti si site sirf aap ke naam.

Aap ki muskurahat, aap ka andaaz aur aap ka acha dil har mehfil ko khoobsurat bana deta hai. Allah aap ko hamesha khush, sehatmand aur kamyab rakhe.

Ye naya saal aap ke liye khushiyan, sukoon aur wo saari kamyabiyan laye jin ke aap haqdaar hain.

Dher saari duaon ke saath, aap ki behna ❤️

Yaadon ki ek jhalak

Aap ke liye meri duaen

  • Sehat: Allah aap ko hamesha tandrust aur tawana rakhe.
  • Kamyabi: Har kaam mein aap ko kamyabi aur izzat naseeb ho.
  • Khushi: Aap ki zindagi mein hamesha muskurahat aur sukoon rahe.
  • Rishte: Aap ke apne hamesha aap ke saath rahein.
